04FAQ

Good to know.

Something else? Open an issue.

Is it really free?

Yes. It’s MIT licensed for personal and commercial work. No paid tier, no ads, no trial. Pricing

Is it safe?

It’s open source, with no ads, no account and no telemetry. Files go straight from the site to your computer. Optional extras like weather and Music Finder only call their own services when you turn them on. Privacy

Will the files open in Premiere?

Yes. Video defaults to H.264 and AAC in an MP4, which Premiere Pro, After Effects and DaVinci Resolve open without converting. Drag the file straight from the app onto your timeline.

What if YouTube breaks it?

yt-dlp updates itself every time the app starts, so fixes usually arrive before you notice. If a download still fails, hit Force Update in Settings, or switch to the nightly channel there.

Do I need to install yt-dlp or FFmpeg?

No. The app sets both up on first launch. If you already have them on your PATH, it uses those.

Can it get playlists or members-only videos?

Playlists, yes: paste the playlist link and it downloads every video. For members-only or age-restricted videos your account can watch, sign in through the Browser tab or let the app use your browser’s cookies in Settings.

Does it work with other sites?

Instagram and Twitch have their own tabs, and the Video tab hands any link to yt-dlp, which supports many more sites. If a page still won’t work, open it in the Browser tab and save the video it finds.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The installer is for Windows 10 and later. On macOS and Linux, clone the repo and run npm install, then npm start.

Is it legal to download videos?

It depends on what you download. Only download content you own or have permission to use, and follow each platform’s terms.
