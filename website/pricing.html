Pricing. It’s free.

Updated November 4, 2026 · Version 1.10.0

The whole desktop app is MIT licensed, for personal and commercial work. There’s no paid tier, no subscription, no in-app purchase and no ads.

$0 Every feature, forever.

What you get

  • Unlimited downloads, run on your own computer with yt-dlp
  • Video up to 4K, audio, subtitles, Instagram and Twitch VODs
  • Video Clipper, Video Divider, Video to GIF and Music Finder
  • yt-dlp and FFmpeg installed and kept up to date for you
  • A Windows installer, or a source build for macOS and Linux

What you don’t get

  • Cloud processing. Everything runs on your machine.
  • A support contract. Help happens in GitHub Issues.
